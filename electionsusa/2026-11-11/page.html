Questions about this average

Who leads the generic congressional ballot?

Democrats lead Republicans 50.8% to 42.8% on the generic congressional ballot, according to the ElectionsUSA average of 119 polls, as of November 3, 2026.

How is this average calculated?

Every poll is weighted by how recent it is (a 14-day half-life), its sample size, the pollster's track record and how often the firm publishes, so a daily tracker can't dominate. The method is fully described on the methodology page and can be reproduced from the open data. Read the method.

Which polls are included?

Every public national poll we can source, 546 on record for this page and 119 carrying weight today. Each row in the table links to the pollster's own release; adult, registered-voter and likely-voter polls all count, but a poll released for several populations counts once (likely voters first).

How often is it updated?

Automatically, four times a day: new polls are collected, checked and added, and the average and chart are recomputed.

Can I use this data?

Yes. Every poll is free to download as CSV, and every average as JSON, under CC BY 4.0. Please credit ElectionsUSA with a link. Download the data.

About this average

The generic ballot asks which party's candidate for Congress a respondent would support. It is a national number and does not map directly onto seats: because of district geography a party usually needs a national lead of a few points to be favoured to win the House.
